Privacy & Legal Notice — RefIntegrity

RefIntegrity is an independent scholarly citation and DOI pre-check utility operating in 0€ Validation Mode (no paid checkout, no user accounts, no payment processing).

1. Local Manuscript Privacy vs Public Registry Queries

  • Your full manuscript, thesis text, or uploaded .bib/.ris files are parsed strictly inside local browser memory and are never uploaded to Conversionlogic servers or stored in localStorage.
  • When you explicitly run an audit, only extracted DOIs and individual bibliographic query strings are sent from your browser to public scholarly registries (api.crossref.org, api.datacite.org, api.openalex.org, and doi.org).
  • No analytics trackers, third-party fonts, advertising pixels, or tracking cookies are loaded.

2. Priority Waitlist Data (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a))

Joining the Pro Batch & CI Toolkit waitlist is strictly optional and requires checking an un-ticked consent box. Your email address and optional workflow preference are used solely to deliver a one-time notification if RefIntegrity Pro launches. You may withdraw consent and request deletion at any time.

3. Informational Verification Scope

RefIntegrity automates multi-registry cross-checks against public Crossref Crossmark, DataCite, OpenAlex, and DOI Registration Agency endpoints. Registry lag, metadata errors, or publisher formatting variations remain possible; results represent an informational verification aid rather than a publisher ruling.
