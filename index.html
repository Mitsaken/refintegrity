Frequently Asked Questions (DOI Auditing & Retraction Checks)

How does RefIntegrity detect retracted papers and fabricated DOIs?

RefIntegrity extracts DOIs (including SICI and DataCite identifiers) and non-DOI bibliographic entries, then queries Crossref (Crossmark update records and title retraction notices), DataCite, OpenAlex, and doi.org Registration Agency records directly from your browser.

Are my manuscript files uploaded to Conversionlogic servers?

No. Your full manuscript text stays in local browser memory. Only extracted DOI identifiers and individual bibliographic citation queries are sent from your browser directly to public scholarly registries (api.crossref.org, api.datacite.org, api.openalex.org, and doi.org).

What is included in the free web tool vs RefIntegrity Pro CLI?

The free browser workbench audits up to 100 citations per batch with CSV, Markdown, JSON, clean BibTeX, and print PDF exports. RefIntegrity Pro (planned 19 EUR one-time launch price, currently 0 EUR Priority Waitlist) adds a standalone batch runner for 1,000+ citations with disk caching, SHA-256 tamper-evident audit manifests, and GitHub Actions PR gates.
